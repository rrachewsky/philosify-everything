# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** September 1, 2026

Philosify (philosify.org) is operated by **Global Goods Corp** ("Philosify", "we"). This policy describes what personal data we collect, why, and what your choices are. It governs alongside the [Terms of Service](/tos).

## 1. What We Collect

**Account data.** E-mail address, name, password (stored as a hash by our authentication provider), preferred language, and — if you sign in with Google — the basic profile Google shares (e-mail, name, avatar). Optionally, a phone number if you use the friend-finding feature; phone numbers you import from your device for matching are compared transiently and never stored.

**Content you create.** Analyses you request, quiz answers, community posts, direct messages, colloquium proposals, and Unsafe Zone conversations. Specific handling for sensitive spaces is described in Sections 4–6.

**Payment data.** Payments are processed entirely by Stripe on Stripe's pages. Card numbers never touch our servers. We store only transaction references: session and price identifiers, a customer reference, and a receipt link.

**Technical data.** IP address (used for rate limiting and fraud prevention), browser user-agent, country, and security event logs. IP addresses recorded with ad impressions are deleted within 48 hours.

## 2. How We Use Data

To operate the Service: run analyses, maintain your credit balance, deliver notifications you enabled, keep the community functioning, prevent fraud and abuse, and comply with law. We do not sell personal data, and we do not use advertising trackers.

## 3. AI Providers

Analyses and dialogues are generated by third-party AI models. What we send them is the content needed for the task — never your name, e-mail, user ID, or IP address:

- **Anthropic (Claude):** Unsafe Zone conversations (exclusively), and some analyses.
- **OpenAI:** some analyses and legacy text-to-speech.
- **Google (Gemini):** some analyses, text-to-speech, quizzes, and translation — including community messages you explicitly ask to translate.
- **xAI (Grok):** some analyses.

These providers process the content under their API terms; per their current API policies, API data is not used to train their models. Their handling is governed by their own privacy terms.

## 4. Unsafe Zone

The Unsafe Zone is built for personal introspection, so it gets specific rules:

- Your conversations are stored on our database so you can resume and revisit them. They are visible to your account only.
- Conversation content is sent exclusively to Anthropic to generate responses, with no identifiers attached.
- **You can delete any session, or all sessions, at any time.** Deletion is physical, not a soft flag.
- We never use Unsafe Zone content for advertising, profiling, or model training, and our systems do not write conversation content to logs.

## 5. Community and Encryption

- **Direct messages** are end-to-end encrypted when both parties have encryption keys (keys are generated in your browser and never leave it). Messages sent without keys are stored readable by the server. Metadata (who talked to whom, when) is always visible to the server to operate the feature.
- **Underground** posts are encrypted at rest: your browser encrypts each post, and our database stores only ciphertext. The room key is generated and held by Philosify (protected by a separate key-encryption key) and delivered over TLS to members who unlocked the space. Posts display your chosen pseudonym to other members — your identity is hidden from fellow members, not from the platform. As the room's operator, Philosify can access a post's content and authorship, and does so only for moderation (when a post is reported) or under legal obligation; every such access is recorded in an audit log.
- **Public and semi-public spaces:** the Ágora chat, colloquium proposals (shown with your display name), the quiz leaderboard (nickname and score), and analyses you share via public link are visible to others by design. Shared analysis links do not expose your identity to viewers.

## 6. Cookies and Local Storage

We use only essential cookies: an authentication session cookie (HttpOnly, 7 days) and a short-lived cookie during Google sign-in. There are no advertising or tracking cookies. Your browser's local storage holds preferences (language, theme) and drafts. Because we use no non-essential cookies, no cookie consent banner is required.

## 7. Error Monitoring

We use Sentry to detect errors and, for a sample of sessions, to replay anonymized interaction sequences (all text masked) so we can fix bugs. Error reports are associated with your user ID only — never your e-mail. Security events (such as blocked suspicious requests) may include the IP address and browser data of the request, used solely for fraud and abuse prevention.

## 8. Notifications

Push notifications use your browser's push service. We store your subscription endpoint and browser identifier; notification contents are held briefly on our servers (deleted within 24–48 hours of delivery). You can disable notifications in your account settings or your browser.

## 9. Service Providers

We share data only with processors needed to run the Service: Supabase (database and authentication), Cloudflare (hosting and delivery), Stripe (payments), Resend (transactional e-mail), the AI providers in Section 3, and content catalogs (Spotify, TMDB, Google Books, news providers) which receive only your search queries. Google Fonts serves our typefaces and receives standard web request data (IP, user-agent).

## 10. Retention

- Unsafe Zone sessions: until you delete them.
- Ad-impression IP addresses: deleted within 48 hours.
- Notification contents: 24–48 hours.
- Account, credit, and transaction records: for the life of your account and as required for accounting and fraud prevention.
- Deleted data may persist in encrypted database backups for a limited period before rotating out.

## 11. Your Rights

You can access and update your profile in your account settings, and delete your content where deletion controls exist (Unsafe Zone sessions, your messages and posts). To exercise other rights — including full account deletion, data access, or correction — e-mail **bob@philosify.org**. Account deletion removes your personal data from our systems; unused credits are forfeited upon deletion, and transaction records required by law are retained. Depending on where you live (including under the LGPD and GDPR), you may have additional statutory rights, which we honor.

## 12. Age

The Service is directed to people aged 16 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16; if we learn we have, we will delete it.

## 13. International Transfers

Our primary database is hosted in South America (São Paulo region). Some of our processors — including payment, e-mail, and AI providers — operate in the United States and other countries, so data sent to them is processed there, with safeguards required by applicable law. Our operating company, Global Goods Corp, is based in the United States.

## 14. Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be announced in the Service. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.
