# PHILOSIFY — GRADE VOCABULARY (canonical, aligned to Guide v3.0 LITE)
**Source of truth for every surface that shows a grade:** verdict blocks, the "About the scale" reference, and all locale translations derive from this file. Aligned to the Guide's §4: the weighted **final_score** (−10.0…+10.0, one decimal; Ethics 40%, Metaphysics 20%, Epistemology 20%, Politics 10%, Aesthetics 10%) maps to the **Philosophical Note (1–10)** and to the **canonical classification enum**.

**Non-Disclosure Rule applies (Guide §0.5):** the meanings below are user-facing — they describe the principles and never name the source philosophy.

**The organizing frame:** each grade names a stance toward five concepts and their opponents — **reality** (a benevolent universe, open to success, in which man holds free will — against evasion, the malevolent-universe premise, determinism and fatalism), **reason** (vs mysticism and skepticism), **self-esteem** (vs altruist guilt and self-sacrifice), **freedom** (vs collectivism and coercion), **beauty** (vs nihilism and ugliness). These are the five axes of the scorecard in conceptual form: metaphysics, epistemology, ethics, politics, aesthetics.

## Structural notes (normative)
1. **The doctrinal grade exists only at the conformist pole.** There is no "Doctrinally Revolutionary": indoctrination means implanting premises by bypassing the audience's judgment, and reason cannot be implanted that way — it must be exercised first-person. Persuasion toward reason is argument, not doctrine. The final word is always yours.
2. **The middle is never neutral.** The two central bands are named by the direction of their lean — toward the Realist or toward Evasion. There is no premise-free ground; a work is always facing somewhere.
3. **Display rule (verdict anatomy):** the big numeral is the **Philosophical Note (1–10)**; beside it the classification (localized display preserving the canonical class); the signed final_score appears as secondary detail; beneath, the per-result rationale (why THIS work earned THIS grade) in smaller reading-tier type — surfaced from the scorecard's weighted justifications and the philosophical_analysis (no new engine fields required).
4. **Classification strings are a canonical enum** (Guide §4.4): localized labels must preserve the meaning exactly and always correspond to the correct canonical class for final_score.
